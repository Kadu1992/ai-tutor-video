# Curriculum Workflow

Read current configuration, state, session history, and the learning contract. Organize phases around observable capabilities and prerequisites. For video-based courses or YouTube playlists, map each video lesson into a sequenced curriculum item with video metadata, duration, and planned concepts. Every checklist item carries a stable topic or lesson ID, required evidence types, and explicit delivery criteria.

For programming, use `references/programming.md`; projects are required only when the capability needs implementation evidence.

Mark completion only from valid evidence IDs and the normative completion rule. Explain proposed roadmap changes and preserve completed history when reprioritizing.
