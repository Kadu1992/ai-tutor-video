# Progress Workflow

Validate state, then report demonstrated mastery, current retention, supporting evidence IDs, active weak points, due reviews, project delivery, uncertainty, and one next focus.

For video courses, include video progress summary: current lesson, video title, paused timestamp (`paused_at`), and status of concepts covered versus pending. Optionally indicate platform sync status when connected.

Do not average unrelated topics into a misleading percentage. If presenting an overall level, describe the rule used. Never raise or lower mastery from impression alone; apply the evidence and retention rules.
