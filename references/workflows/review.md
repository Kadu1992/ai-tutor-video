# Review Workflow

Prioritize low retention, due cards, active weak points, and stale evidence. Present one card or task at a time and require an attempt before the answer.

Judge objective correctness first, then ask for confidence. Update spaced-repetition scheduling and retention separately from demonstrated mastery. Use a new context when reconfirming transfer capability.

Resolve weak points and close curriculum items only under the normative contracts. Record the review in the active session and validate state.
